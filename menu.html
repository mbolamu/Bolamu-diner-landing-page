Bolamu Diner Menu

Mbika-scoff

You've heard of Biscoff. Now meet Mbika-Scoff. A rich cookie butter packed with Congolese flavours, blending mbika, a traditional delicacy made from roasted egusi melon seeds, with a quintessential West African spice blend and caramelised biscuit notes into something comfortingly familiar and completely new.

Pili Pili Coco Noir

Meet Nutella's Congolese cousin. Roasted hazelnut butter meets deep dark chocolate, wood-smoked sea salt and a gentle spark of Scotch bonnet. Rich, bold and just dangerous enough to keep you coming back for another bite.

Hypermalt Crème Molasses

Whether it's Guinness or Supermalt, no Congolese meal feels quite complete without a crisp fizzy malt drink. This flavour blends roasted malt, stout notes and buttery molasses into a rich, creamy caramel layered with hints of toffee.

Safu Coco Blanc

A decadent homage to safu, Africa's beloved butter pear. This rare delicacy has a uniquely distinctive flavour that combines the silky richness of avocado with the bright tang of a citrus fruit, creating a taste unlike anything you've experienced before. Blended with creamy white chocolate, the result is fruity, silky and surprisingly luxurious.

Mbila Doré

What if hot honey was made in Congo? Mbila, better known as palm nut, is the fruit responsible for the distinctive tang found in the red palm oil used in almost every Congolese dish. This flavour blends palm fruit pulp, golden honey and Scotch bonnet into a sweet heat sensation that starts smooth, finishes warm and leaves you reaching for one more spoonful.
