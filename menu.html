Bolamu Diner Menu

Swipe through the flavour collection and tap the arrow beneath a card to discover its story.

Mbika-scoff

You've heard of Biscoff. Now meet Mbika-scoff. A rich cookie butter inspired by Central African flavours, blending roasted egusi, warm spices and caramelised biscuit notes into something comfortingly familiar and completely new.

Pili Pili Coco Noir

Deep dark chocolate meets smoky sea salt, roasted hazelnut and a gentle spark of Scotch bonnet. Rich, bold and just dangerous enough to keep you coming back for another bite.

Hypermalt Crème Molasses

Inspired by the malty drinks found across African households, this flavour blends roasted malt, stout notes and buttery caramel into a rich, velvety dessert experience.

Safu Coco Blanc

Creamy white chocolate meets safu, Africa's beloved butter pear. Fruity, silky and surprisingly luxurious, it's a flavour unlike anything you've tasted before.

Mbila Doré

Golden honey, palm fruit and Scotch bonnet combine in a sweet heat sensation that starts smooth, finishes warm and leaves you reaching for one more spoonful.
