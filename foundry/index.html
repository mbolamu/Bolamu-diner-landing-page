Across Congo, opportunity has rarely arrived with an invitation.

It has been built.

From market stalls and roadside grills to neighbourhood workshops and family kitchens, generations have created businesses with whatever was within reach. For many Congolese families, entrepreneurship has never simply been a career choice.

It has been a way of creating possibility.

The Foundry is Bolamu’s celebration of that spirit. A place where it is forged into products for the next generation of makers. Here you’ll discover ingredients designed to help restaurants create something original, premixes that shorten the distance between an idea and a business, and flavours that invite home cooks, cafés, restaurants and entrepreneurs to build something of their own.

The forge is still hot.
